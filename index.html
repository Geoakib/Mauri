The Return of Jesus ('Isa, Peace Be Upon Him) to Earth

One of the strongest bonds between Muslims and Christians is the shared conviction that Jesus the Messiah (peace be upon him) is alive and will physically return to earth. In Sunni Islam—grounded strictly in the Noble Quran and consecutive authentic Hadith (Mutawatir) transmitted by the noble Companions—his return is a major sign of the Last Hour and a triumph of truth, peace, and justice.

"By Him in Whose Hands my soul is, surely (Jesus,) the son of Mary (peace be upon him) will soon descend amongst you and will judge mankind justly (as a just ruler); he will break the Cross and kill the pigs and there will be no Jizya (taxation taken from non-Muslims). Money will be in abundance so that nobody will accept it, and a single prostration to Allah (in prayer) will be better than the whole world and whatever is in it."

— Prophet Muhammad (ﷺ), narrated by Abu Hurairah (may Allah be pleased with him) in Sahih al-Bukhari 3448 & Sahih Muslim 155a

Why We Follow the Quran, Authentic Hadith & Honor All Companions

Mainstream Islam—followed by roughly 90% of the world's Muslims (Ahlus Sunnah wal Jama'ah, or Sunni Islam)—rests directly on two clear, preserved foundations:

📘 1. The Noble Quran

The verbatim word of God revealed to Prophet Muhammad (ﷺ), which confirms the truth of the original revelations given to Abraham, Moses, and Jesus (peace be upon them all)—including the original Gospel (Al-Injil, Quran 5:46)—and acts as the final, preserved criterion (Quran 5:48) over what came before it.

📜 2. The Authentic Sunnah (Hadith)

The verified teachings, prayers, and example of Prophet Muhammad (ﷺ), preserved in collections like Sahih al-Bukhari and Sahih Muslim through unbroken chains of trustworthy eyewitnesses (Isnad).

🕊️ Honoring Both the Disciples of Jesus & All Companions of Muhammad (ﷺ)

In the Quran (Surah Ali 'Imran 3:52 & Surah As-Saff 61:14), God praises the faithful Disciples of Jesus (Al-Hawariyyun) who stood by him and said: "We are supporters of Allah; we have believed in Allah and testify that we are submitting to Him."

In the exact same spirit, Sunni Muslims love, respect, and pray for all the noble Companions (Sahabah) and pure household (Ahlul Bayt) of Prophet Muhammad (ﷺ)—including Abu Bakr as-Siddiq, 'Umar ibn al-Khattab, 'Uthman ibn 'Affan, 'Ali ibn Abi Talib, Mother of the Believers 'Aishah, Fatimah, al-Hasan, al-Husayn, and Abu Hurairah (may Allah be pleased with them all)—following the Quranic praise in Surah At-Tawbah 9:100 and Surah Al-Hashr 59:10, and never speaking negatively about any of them.

🌊 Caring for Our Islands & Living in Kindness With Neighbors

Islam teaches that human beings are entrusted by God as caretakers and stewards of the earth (Surah Al-Baqarah 2:30)—protecting the oceans, trees, and fragile island ecosystems—and commands Muslims to deal kindly, justly, and respectfully with peaceful Christian neighbors (Surah Al-Mumtahanah 60:8).
