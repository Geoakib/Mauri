Why We Follow the Quran, Authentic Hadith & Honor All Companions

Mainstream Islam—followed by roughly 90% of the world's Muslims (Ahlus Sunnah wal Jama'ah, or Sunni Islam)—rests directly on two clear, preserved foundations:

📘 1. The Noble Quran

The verbatim word of God revealed to Prophet Muhammad (ﷺ), which confirms the truth of the earlier revelations given to Abraham, Moses, and Jesus (peace be upon them all) and calls humanity to worship the One Creator directly without intermediaries.

📜 2. The Authentic Sunnah (Hadith)

The verified teachings, prayers, and example of Prophet Muhammad (ﷺ), preserved in collections like Sahih al-Bukhari and Sahih Muslim through rigorous chains of eyewitness verification.

🕊️ Honoring Both the Disciples of Jesus & the Companions of Muhammad (ﷺ)

In the Quran (Surah Ali 'Imran 3:52), God praises the faithful disciples of Jesus (Al-Hawariyyun) who stood by him and said: "We are supporters of Allah; we have believed in Allah and testify that we are submitting to Him."

In the exact same spirit, Sunni Muslims love, respect, and pray for all the Companions (Sahabah) and family of Prophet Muhammad (ﷺ)—including Abu Bakr, 'Umar, 'Uthman, 'Ali, Mother of the Believers 'Aishah, and Abu Hurairah (may Allah be pleased with them all)—and never speak negatively about any of the men and women who sacrificed to preserve God's message for humanity.
